About

I am a Lecturer in the Department of Electrical and Electronic Engineering at BRAC University, Dhaka, and an M.Sc. student at the Bangladesh University of Engineering and Technology (BUET), where only the thesis defense remains. My research focuses on silicon photonics and photonic inverse design. I develop adjoint topology-optimized silicon-on-insulator devices using FDTD simulation, and for my thesis I study quantum sensing with VB− spin defects in hexagonal boron nitride (h-BN), including adjoint-optimized photonic collection structures that aim to enhance spin-readout efficiency.

As an undergraduate researcher at BUET, I used ab initio DFT simulations to study ultrascaled two-dimensional field-effect transistors, which led to a journal article in IEEE Transactions on Electron Devices. Alongside research, I teach digital electronics, signals and systems, and an energy conversion laboratory, and I have hands-on experience in analog circuit design with Cadence Virtuoso and in FPGA-based digital design.

I am looking for a fully funded PhD position.

Research

The main threads of my work, from my M.Sc. thesis back to my undergraduate research.

Quantum sensing with VB− spin defects in hexagonal boron nitride

M.Sc. thesis BUET · Nov 2024 – present · Supervisor: Dr. Kawsar Alam

My thesis aims to improve the sensitivity of defect-based h-BN quantum sensors. I investigate optically detected magnetic resonance (ODMR) of VB− spin defects for quantum magnetometry, and I explore adjoint-optimized photonic collection structures that enhance spin-readout efficiency.

  • h-BN
  • VB− spin defects
  • ODMR
  • Quantum magnetometry
  • Adjoint optimization

Adjoint-optimized all-silicon refractive index sensor

Manuscript in preparation BUET · silicon-on-insulator, 220 nm

I designed and inversely optimized a compact all-silicon refractive index sensor using adjoint-based topology optimization with FDTD simulation in Tidy3D. The optimized device reaches competitive sensitivity, quality factor and figure of merit on a 220 nm SOI platform.

  • Silicon photonics
  • Topology optimization
  • FDTD (Tidy3D)
  • Refractive index sensing

Ultrascaled 2-D transistors: monolayer α-CS and SiP

Published Undergraduate research, BUET · Aug 2023 – Jul 2024

Using ab initio DFT simulations in QuantumATK, I studied n-type field-effect transistors based on monolayer α-CS and SiP, and investigated underlayer modulation strategies for scaling performance beyond the 5-nm technology node. The work appeared in IEEE Transactions on Electron Devices (2025).

  • DFT
  • QuantumATK
  • 2-D materials
  • Beyond 5-nm FETs

Field-free and toggle SOT switching in PMA MRAM

M.Sc. course project (EEE 6411, BUET; team of five). We simulated field-assisted, field-free and toggle spin-orbit-torque switching in a PMA MRAM cell using the LLGS equation, checked against a reference paper; the toggle range peaked at β = 3–4. I worked on the LLGS model and the field-assisted cases.

  • SOT-MRAM
  • Spintronics
  • LLGS
  • Macromagnetic simulation
